Documents

Every registered document with its versions and files. Select a row to open it.

Expiring

Documents that have expired or will within 30 days, the soonest first, such as business permits. Renew one with its new file and new dates; the old version stays in its history.

Users

The people who can sign in. You make each account and set its first password; the person chooses their own the first time they sign in. Nobody else changes a password.

Roles

A role says what a person may do in each department. Make a role, then tick, per department, what it may do. A person has one role (choose it on the Users screen). Administrators can do everything and are not edited.

Departments

The departments documents can belong to. A department is switched off, never deleted: its documents keep it, it just is not offered for new documents.

Archiving

Old versions that nobody opens, packed into a zip to save space. An archived file still opens and downloads, only slower. A current version can never be archived.

Audits

Every change to a document, version, file or account: who made it, when, and the value before and after. Nothing here can be edited or removed.
